Industry News Aggregation Inquiries

RC Pit Spec aggregates new product announcements, race information, and other public news from RC manufacturers and media outlets through the "Industry News" feature (v2.0.1+). We collect only titles, public URLs, and publication dates; we do not collect article bodies or images. Tapping a news item redirects users to the official source page.

If you wish to opt out of aggregation, or have inquiries regarding aggregated content, please contact us at info@kgapplab.com. We will stop aggregating the requested source within 24 hours of receipt.
